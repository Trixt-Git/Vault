The Pitch Core: Engine vs. Tracks

Concept Physical Explanation for Stakeholders
The Engine (Vault Core) The permanent software machinery: document parsing, database vector/text search, Pydantic validation, human approval screens, and audit traces. Built once.
The Track (Route A) The concrete demonstration through-line shown across Stages 1–8. Proves schemas, latency, and human control using Estimating so buyers inspect real wiring.
The Switch (Stage 0) The Discovery Gate. The team measures the actual plant floor. Leadership chooses which track to build first based on real friction, not vendor guesses.
Executive Soundbite

"Vault is the operational memory engine for your entire plant. In this pitch, we walk through Route A—Estimating—step-by-step so you can inspect every wire and human approval screen. But in Stage 0, we don't assume estimating is your biggest bottleneck. We measure your floor, and you decide which track we pave first."
