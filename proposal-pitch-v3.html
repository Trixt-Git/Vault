THE GARY PROBLEM

Every shop has a Gary.

A tough request comes in. Nobody searches the folder; they ask Gary. Gary glances up: "We ran that exact spec for Hendricks in August—five thousand on 100-pound gloss. Call it eighteen-fifty." And he's right.

Gary finally gets to take a vacation. Vault is Gary’s memory, with the proof attached. The desk still makes the call.

STEP 03

WHAT COULD GO SIDEWAYS

We know where the bumps are.

  • Messy old specs2021 records describe stock differently than 2025 records. Discovery measures cleanup cost upfront.
  • Yesterday's pricesStock costs shift. Old matches flag as historical reference, never current pricing.
  • The customer factorContracts and relationships alter final rates. Vault provides the baseline; the desk sets the price.
  • Zero matchesWhen history comes up blank, Vault admits it and routes the request to a human.

What Vault will never do.

  • Send anything to a customer autonomously.
  • Alter system records or pricing tables.
  • Overrule the person on the desk.

Every edge-case failure logs expected vs. actual behavior, root cause, and fix.
